Important notes

Shortcuts can differ between InPage versions, keyboard layouts and selected tools. Some commands may work only when a text box or document is active. Confirm any unfamiliar shortcut in your own InPage version before relying on it.

To switch between Urdu and English, Ctrl + Space is commonly used when the required language/keyboard is enabled in InPage preferences.
